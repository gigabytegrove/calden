# CalDen 0.1.0-alpha10

This release expands Bill Pay from due-date tracking into simple household payment history without turning CalDen into a budgeting or banking app.

## Monthly paid status

Bill Pay events can now be marked paid for a specific occurrence.

- Recurring bills keep independent paid status for each month/occurrence.
- Marking October's electric bill paid does not mark November's bill paid.
- Non-recurring bills keep their payment status if their due date is moved.
- Deleting a recurring bill occurrence clears that occurrence's payment status.

## Assigned payer vs. actual payer

CalDen now distinguishes between:

- **Assigned payer** — who was expected to handle the bill.
- **Actual payer** — who really paid it.

The actual payer can be any active CalDen user and does not need to match the assigned payer.

When marking a bill paid, CalDen records:

- who actually paid it
- when it was marked paid
- the amount actually paid

The payment amount starts with the bill's expected amount, but it can be changed when a variable or estimated bill came in at a different amount.

## Bill Pay dashboard

The monthly Bill Pay view now shows:

- expected total
- known/fixed amounts
- estimated amounts
- amount paid so far
- outstanding amount
- paid vs. outstanding bill counts
- expected responsibility by assigned person
- expected totals by bill calendar
- actual payments grouped by the person who paid them

Each bill row shows whether it is due or paid, the actual payer, the actual paid amount, and when it was marked paid.

Paid status also appears on normal Calendar and Agenda views for Bill Pay events.

## Payment controls

Editable Bill Pay calendars now include a dedicated payment workflow.

- **Mark paid** opens a payment dialog.
- The assigned payer is shown for reference.
- **Paid by** can be changed to another household member.
- **Amount actually paid** can be adjusted from the expected amount.
- Existing payment details can be changed later.
- **Mark unpaid** removes that occurrence's paid record without changing the bill itself.

Calendar edit permissions continue to control who may change payment state.

## Data model

Payment history is stored separately from the recurring bill definition.

This keeps recurring bill metadata such as expected amount and assigned payer on the series while storing actual payment state by occurrence. It also lets historical months continue to show who actually paid what.

## Validation

CI verifies:

- an assigned bill can be paid by a different person
- the actual paid amount can differ from the estimated amount
- paid-by-person monthly totals use the actual payer and amount
- one occurrence of a recurring monthly bill can be paid without changing the next occurrence
- a paid occurrence can be returned to unpaid
- the existing Bill Pay, notification, recurring-event, Google migration, iCalendar, backup/restore, Tailwind UI, and Docker Raw deployment tests remain enabled


## Google Calendar duplicate-event repair

A real Google Calendar export exposed an important Google Takeout behavior: the same event UID can be present in more than one exported calendar file after an event was moved between calendars. Importing every file independently makes one logical event appear multiple times in CalDen.

CalDen now:

- reads Google `LAST-MODIFIED`, `DTSTAMP`, `CREATED`, and `SEQUENCE` revision metadata
- treats the same UID across multiple Google calendar files as the same logical event
- keeps the newest revision in the calendar that owns the current copy
- suppresses older stale copies before import
- reports how many duplicate event copies were suppressed in the preview and import results
- on re-import, reconciles duplicate event rows created by older CalDen imports
- preserves CalDen assignees and non-duplicate reminders when consolidating those rows
- preserves Bill Pay metadata/payment history when the surviving destination is still a Bill Pay calendar
- does not guess when duplicate copies have identical revision metadata

The same Google export can therefore be re-imported after this update to repair duplicates created by earlier builds.
