# CalDen 0.1.0-alpha11

Alpha11 fixes the existing Google-import duplicate problem in place and expands Bill Pay into a proper monthly payment ledger.

## Repair existing Google-import duplicates without deleting calendars

Older CalDen imports could store the same Google event UID more than once when Google Takeout represented one logical event in more than one source calendar file. Google Calendar itself does not need to show duplicate events for this to happen; the duplication was created in CalDen's database during import.

Alpha11 adds a dedicated repair workflow under **Integrations → Google Calendar**.

- **Scan CalDen** finds duplicate parent event rows that share the same imported Google UID.
- The original Google export is used only as a reference to determine which existing CalDen row is the canonical/current copy.
- Preview shows how many duplicate groups and redundant CalDen rows are repairable before anything is changed.
- Repair consolidates CalDen-side data and deletes only redundant event rows.
- Existing calendars are not deleted.
- Nonduplicate events are not deleted.
- The repair action does not re-import the Google export.
- Assignees, reminders, notifications, and Bill Pay metadata are preserved when the surviving event supports them.
- Old auto-created Google calendars and current mapped calendars are both recognized when scanning existing data.
- Future Google imports continue to suppress stale duplicate UID copies before creating events.

This means an existing Alpha10.1 installation with a duplicated calendar does **not** need to delete calendars and start over.

## Bill Pay: no balance, partial payments, and split payments

Bill activity is now a ledger for each individual bill occurrence.

A month can be:

- **Due**
- **Partial**
- **Paid**
- **No balance**

Each payment entry stores:

- who actually paid it
- amount paid
- the date the payment was made
- an optional cleared date
- whether that payment completes the bill

Multiple payments can be recorded against the same month's bill, including payments made by different household members.

The assigned payer remains separate from the actual payer history.

## Due date vs. payment date vs. cleared date

CalDen now keeps these concepts separate:

- **Due date** comes from the calendar event.
- **Payment date** records when money was actually sent or paid.
- **Cleared date** is optional and records when the payment cleared the bank, card, processor, or biller.

The Bill Pay list and payment dialog show these independently instead of treating “marked paid” time as the payment date.

## No balance this month

Recurring bills can now be marked **No balance this month** without deleting or changing the recurring bill itself.

No-balance status applies only to that occurrence. The next month remains due normally.

## Payment history and monthly totals

The Bill Pay dashboard now accounts for partial and split payments correctly.

- **Paid so far** is the sum of individual payment entries.
- **Outstanding** subtracts recorded partial payments from known amounts.
- **Paid by person** groups actual payment entries, not the person originally assigned to the bill.
- Bill rows show Due, Partial, Paid, or No balance.
- The payment dialog includes the full month's payment history with edit/delete controls.

## Payment modal UI fix

The Bill Pay payment dialog no longer creates its own horizontal overflow. The dialog and all payment controls are constrained to the viewport and reflow on smaller screens.

## Compatibility

The Alpha10 single-payment endpoint remains available for older clients. The Alpha11 web UI and future mobile clients use the multi-payment API.

## Validation

CI covers:

- partial payment followed by a final split payment
- different actual payers for the same bill
- actual payment date and cleared date
- no-balance status on one recurring occurrence without affecting another
- paid-by-person split totals
- no horizontal scrollbar in the payment modal
- scanning duplicate Google-imported rows already stored in CalDen
- previewing repair against the original export
- repairing those rows in place without deleting any calendars
- existing Google import, recurrence, notifications, backup/restore, Tailwind UI, and Docker Raw deployment paths
