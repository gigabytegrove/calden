# Calden 1.4.3 — Appointments stay on the date you choose

## What changed

If you save an appointment several months ahead, the calendar now takes you to that appointment's date instead of leaving you looking at the current month.

Calden also double-checks the saved date and time. If the recorded date doesn't match what you entered, you get a warning rather than a misleading success message. This check also applies when you reschedule one appointment in a repeating series.

Notifications now make it clearer that **Notified** means when the message arrived, **not** when the appointment happens.

We tested the exact example of an appointment on **January 13, 2027**, which could otherwise be confused with the notification date, **October 13, 2026**.

## What you need to do

Install 1.4.3 from **Settings → Updates** if you haven't already. You do not need to recreate your appointments. Your existing calendars and events are kept.

**Android:** Its corresponding date and notification-sound improvements are in the Android development branch and will require a separately verified Android build.
