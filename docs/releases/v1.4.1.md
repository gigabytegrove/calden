# CalDen 1.4.1

## Household Tasks & Chores
- Recurring daily, weekly, every-two-week, monthly and annual chores with optional end dates.
- Multiple people can share a task; every participant independently marks each occurrence Pending, In progress or Done.
- Edit and delete the entire chore series while retaining completed occurrences for continuing assignments.
- Existing single-person chores and progress migrate without data loss.
- Add the missing Tasks & Chores sidebar icon and refine the task editor.

## Optional navigation
- Household administrators can show or hide Bills and Agenda independently under Settings → Household (or Android Settings).
- These are visibility controls; disabling them never deletes bills, calendars, payments, event records or reminders.

## Devices and ownership
- Administrators can review registered devices under each person in People.
- Android Settings shows devices grouped by their assigned household user.
- Device listings do not expose installation secrets or push notification tokens.
- Own-account device revocation remains available under Settings → My devices in the browser.

## Upgrade notes
- Migration 026 is additive. Back up the database before upgrading.
- Update the CalDen server before installing Android 1.4.1 so both clients have the new API.
