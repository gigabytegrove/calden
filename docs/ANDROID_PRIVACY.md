# Calden for Android — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 22, 2026_

Calden for Android connects to a Calden household server selected by the user. The Android app does not provide a mandatory central hosted Calden account. Your household's server administrator controls the server's data, access and retention policies.

## Information used to operate the app

The Android app may process and display household account details, calendar events, assigned family members, appointments, tasks and chores, reminders, notification messages, and bill/payment-tracking information that is available to your account. It sends your actions and changes to your configured Calden server.

The app stores your server address, a login session token, preferences and an app installation identifier in Android-private storage. The application disables Android system backup of its local data, including its session. Logging out removes the local login token; Android's app-data controls can remove other device-local settings.

## Notifications and optional Firebase service

Calden can deliver local reminders and notifications using Android notification and scheduling features. If the server administrator enables Firebase Cloud Messaging, the app may obtain a push-notification token from Google's Firebase service and register that token, its installation ID and device make/model with your chosen Calden server. Firebase and Google may process technical information and message payloads required for push notifications under their own policies. Firebase is optional, and the server supplies its configuration.

Notification content can appear on the device's notification surfaces according to Android's notification settings. Some notifications may include appointment, event or household information.

## Security and network access

The Android app connects to the server address you enter. Use HTTPS over the public internet; the app also supports HTTP for private local-network servers. Communication and retention on the server are controlled by the server operator. Do not assume data is end-to-end encrypted between household members.

## Third-party analytics, advertising and sales

The app does not include an advertising SDK. It does not sell personal data. Google Play, Android, Firebase when configured, and any other identity/network services configured by your server may process information under their respective policies.

## Deleting data and accounts

You may sign out, uninstall the app, or clear local app data in Android Settings. These actions do not delete family events, account records or server backups. To request server-side account or household data deletion, contact the administrator of the Calden server you use. The administrator is responsible for its backups and retention controls.
